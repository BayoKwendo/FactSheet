## Full description (max 4000 characters)
HIVFactSheet is a free, offline-first guide to HIV, reproductive health and youth wellbeing — written for Kenya and kept up to date with current global guidance (WHO, CDC, UNAIDS) and Kenya's NASCOP protocols.

Whether you want to understand HIV, get tested, start or stay on treatment, protect yourself with PrEP, or learn about sexual and reproductive health, it's all in one easy, private app that works without internet.

WHAT YOU GET

• Clear, up-to-date HIV information — testing, treatment (TLD / dolutegravir), U=U (Undetectable = Untransmittable), PrEP and PEP, CD4 and viral load explained simply.

• Reproductive & sexual health — anatomy, puberty and menstruation, contraception and family planning, consent, gender, teenage pregnancy, and sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV) support.

• Find a health centre near you — see nearby HIV Comprehensive Care Centres (CCCs) and hospitals sorted by distance, with one tap to open directions in Maps. Works offline using a built-in facility list.

• Powerful search — find any topic instantly.

• Built for everyone — read-aloud (Listen), adjustable text size, light and dark mode, and bookmarks to save pages for later.

• Private & offline — the content lives on your device, no account is needed, and most features work with no internet or data.

ALWAYS CURRENT

Health information is reviewed against the latest guidance, so you won't see outdated advice: test-and-treat and same-day ART, U=U, modern PrEP options (including long-acting), post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) within 72 hours, PMTCT for pregnancy and breastfeeding, and more.

WHO IT'S FOR

Young people, people living with HIV, partners and families, peer educators, counsellors, and anyone who wants trustworthy sexual and reproductive health information.

IMPORTANT

This app is for education and does not replace advice from a qualified health worker. If you need care, please visit a health facility.
