HIVFactSheet — Privacy Policy

HIV & Reproductive Health education app

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Developer: Brian Kwendo · Contact: hconnect254@gmail.com

HIVFactSheet (“the app”) is a free educational app about HIV, reproductive health and youth wellbeing. We respect your privacy. The app is designed to work offline and does not require you to create an account.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. All educational content is stored on your device.

Location

The Find a Health Center feature can show health facilities near you. If you grant location permission, your device location is used only to calculate the distance to nearby facilities and is processed on your device. To fetch an up-to-date list of nearby clinics, your approximate coordinates may be sent to the public OpenStreetMap (Overpass) service purely to answer that request; we do not store this information. Opening directions launches Google Maps. If you do not grant location permission, the rest of the app works normally, and the app also includes a built-in offline list of facilities.

Internet access

The app uses the internet only to (a) look up nearby health facilities and (b) open external links such as Google Maps, YouTube and public health dashboards. No personal data is transmitted to the developer.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share your personal data. We operate no servers that store your data.

Permissions used

  • Location (approximate/precise) — to find health facilities near you.
  • Internet / network state — to fetch the clinic list and open external links.

Third-party services

The app relies on OpenStreetMap (Overpass API), Google Maps, and Google Play Services (for app updates and the system “turn on location” prompt). These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

The app provides health education suitable for young people and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
